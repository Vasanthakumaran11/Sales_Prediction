## 🎨 Color Coding

The application uses ANSI colors for clarity:

- 🔵 **BLUE**: Information and headers
- 🟢 **GREEN**: Success messages and confirmations
- 🔴 **RED**: Error messages
- 🟡 **YELLOW**: Warnings
- 🔵 **CYAN**: Highlights and numbers

---

## 🎯 Risk Level Indicators

When managing inventory:

- 🟢 **LOW**: Good stock levels (> 100 days supply)
- 🟡 **MEDIUM**: Normal operations (30-100 days)
- 🔴 **HIGH**: Reorder recommended (10-30 days)
- 🔴🔴 **CRITICAL**: Urgent action needed (< 10 days)

---

## 📋 Form Features

All forms include:
- ✅ **Input Validation**: Ensures correct data type
- ✅ **Default Values**: Helpful auto-fill (e.g., today's date)
- ✅ **Dropdown Options**: Easy selection (locations, product types)
- ✅ **Error Messages**: Clear guidance on invalid input
- ✅ **Confirmation**: Review before saving
- ✅ **Auto-Calculation**: Revenue computed automatically
